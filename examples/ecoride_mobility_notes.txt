EcoRide Mobility is an early revenue-generating electric mobility startup operating in Pune. Its products include hourly electric scooter rentals and monthly subscription plans, with fleet servicing and charging partnerships as supporting operations.

The company serves students, young professionals, and delivery workers. It plans to evaluate expansion to Mumbai and Bengaluru with an available expansion budget of ₹2 crore. Its goals are to achieve disciplined city launch economics, grow recurring subscription revenue, and maintain safe fleet utilization.

Known competitors include local scooter-rental operators, ride-hailing alternatives, and personal vehicle ownership. Current challenges include uncertain demand, intense competition, limited digital marketing capacity, capital discipline, fleet scaling, charging access, EV regulation, and compliance requirements.
